PERSONAL FINANCE PLANNING APP — PRODUCT BRIEF v1.0
===================================================
Platform: iOS (SwiftUI)
Distribution: TestFlight initially
Users: Solo or household (2 people) — configured at setup
Currency: Euros (€)


OVERVIEW
--------
A big picture personal finance planning app that replaces a complex Google Sheets budget tracker. The core purpose is to make monthly financial planning intuitive, flexible, and easy to maintain — with the ability to zoom out and see the full year. Built for one person or a household of two, with variable incomes and real life surprise expenses in mind.

The problem it solves: planned budgets never match reality because unexpected expenses pop up and the current spreadsheet is too rigid and tedious to update. This app makes replanning easy and handles surprises gracefully.


BUDGETING PHILOSOPHY
--------------------
At setup, the user chooses a budgeting framework. The app recommends one based on their situation but any can be selected:

• 50/30/20 — Needs / Wants / Savings (default recommendation)
• 70/20/10 — more relaxed, less savings pressure
• Zero-based — assign every euro a job
• Pay yourself first — savings deducted first, spend the rest
• Custom — user sets their own percentages

The chosen framework is the backbone of all planning and reporting. Percentages are shown in real time as the user plans.


HOUSEHOLD SETUP
---------------
At onboarding the user chooses:
• Solo mode — one person's income and expenses
• Household mode — two people (e.g. Guppy + Chappy), each with their own income and accounts, plus a joint account

In household mode:
• Each person's income is entered separately
• Expenses are tagged as Person 1, Person 2, or Joint
• The joint account is tracked separately
• Both individual and household totals are visible


INCOME
------
• Each person's income is entered separately each month
• Income is variable — no fixed amount assumed
• Multiple income sources per person supported (e.g. main job + side income)
• Total household income calculated automatically


EXPENSE STRUCTURE
-----------------
Expenses are organised into the three buckets of the chosen framework (e.g. Needs / Wants / Savings). Within each bucket, categories are customisable. 

Default categories based on current usage:

Needs:
• Rent
• Gas
• Strom (electricity)
• Internet
• Rundfunkbeitrag
• Phone bills (per person)
• Insurance (per person)
• Groceries
• Semester fees
• DE-Ticket
• Liability insurance
• Berliner Mieterverein
• Medical / Healing (Derm, Psychiatrist, Psychotherapy)
• Sachiko & Beanie expenses (insurance, food, vet)

Wants:
• Leisure
• Personal money (per person)
• Skincare
• Travel
• Wellness (badminton, gym, etc.)
• Donations
• Subscriptions (iCloud, Spotify, ChatGPT, Google, N26, etc.)

Savings & Investments:
• Savings
• Investments (tracked by type — stocks, property, etc.)


INSTALMENT TRACKING
-------------------
One-off large expenses can be split across multiple months:
• User enters total amount + number of months to spread it across
• App automatically allocates the monthly portion
• Shown clearly as an instalment in the monthly plan
• Examples: flight tickets, Airbnb deposits, Faroe Islands trip, Chiko wheel, glasses


TENTATIVE / PROSPECTIVE EXPENSES
---------------------------------
• Users can add future or unconfirmed expenses marked as "tentative"
• Tentative expenses are visible in planning but flagged differently from confirmed ones
• Can be confirmed or removed as the month approaches
• Yearly expenses (e.g. Berliner Mieterverein, liability insurance) can be entered once and distributed across the relevant months automatically


SURPRISE EXPENSE HANDLING
--------------------------
When an unexpected expense comes up mid-month:
• User logs the expense and tags it to a category
• App shows the impact on the affected bucket
• App suggests where the money could come from (e.g. "take from leisure budget" or "reduce travel allocation this month")
• User makes the final decision
• Plan updates automatically to reflect the change


TIME VIEWS
----------
The app supports three views:
• Monthly — plan and track one month at a time
• Multi-month — see several months side by side
• Full year — zoom out to see the entire year at a glance

In all views, both planned and actual figures are shown side by side.


PLANNED VS ACTUAL
-----------------
• Every expense category shows planned amount vs actual spent
• Variance is highlighted (over/under)
• Bucket-level percentages shown in real time (e.g. "currently 58% on Needs")
• App flags when a bucket is overspending against the chosen framework


UNSPENT BUDGET
--------------
At the end of each month, any unspent budget per category is surfaced. The app suggests what to do with it (e.g. roll into savings, carry forward, allocate to a goal) and the user decides.


SAVINGS & INVESTMENTS
---------------------
• Savings and investments tracked separately
• Investments broken down by type (e.g. stocks, property)
• Target percentages set at setup based on chosen framework
• Progress shown against targets month by month and year to date


YEARLY EXPENSES
---------------
Some expenses occur annually or quarterly rather than monthly:
• Can be entered once with their recurrence schedule
• App distributes them across the correct months automatically
• Examples: Rundfunkbeitrag (quarterly), liability insurance (annual), semester fees (per semester)


NAVIGATION
----------

The app has 4 tabs:

1. HOME
   The current month at a glance. Live tracking of how the month is going — income, bucket percentages, planned vs actual. Includes a persistent "Something came up?" button that launches the surprise expense flow without leaving the screen.

2. PLANNING
   The monthly sit-down. Allocate income across categories, set budgets, manage instalments, configure savings and investments. This is where you plan the month before it starts and adjust mid-month when needed. Also includes buffer / emergency pot allocation and tentative / prospective expenses.

3. REVIEW
   All history in one place. See any past month's performance — planned vs actual, bucket breakdowns, percentage splits. Zoom out to full year view to see trends across months. Locked data — no editing, just reviewing.

4. SETTINGS
   App configuration. Household or solo setup, budgeting framework selection, category management, account setup.


SECONDARY SCREENS (accessed from within tabs)
----------------------------------------------
• Onboarding — household or solo setup, income entry, framework selection
• Surprise expense handler — log unexpected cost, see impact, app suggests reallocation options, user decides
• Add / edit category — create or adjust a budget category
• Add instalment — enter large expense, set number of months to split across
• Yearly / recurring expense setup — enter annual or quarterly expenses, app distributes across correct months
• Add tentative expense — flag a future or unconfirmed cost
• Buffer setup — allocate monthly reserve for unknowns like Nachzahlung


WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT FROM THE SPREADSHEET
---------------------------------------------
• Variable income handled gracefully — replanning is easy each month
• Surprise expenses don't break the plan — app guides you through reallocation
• Real time percentages — always know which bucket you're in
• Instalment tracking built in — no manual splitting across months
• Zoom in and out — monthly detail or full year view
• Planned vs actual always visible — no hunting through rows and columns
